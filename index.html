FAQ

Common
Questions

Do I need an appointment? +
No appointment needed for most repairs. Just walk in to 419 Main St during business hours. If you'd like to call ahead, you can reach us at (973) 558-3576.
How long do repairs take? +
Most repairs — including screen replacements, battery swaps, and charge port fixes — are completed in under an hour while you wait. More complex repairs may take longer; we'll always give you an honest estimate upfront.
Do you fix all phone brands? +
Yes! We repair iPhones, Samsung, Google Pixel, LG, Motorola, and most other major Android brands. We're Apple and Samsung certified, so you're in good hands no matter what device you bring in.
What if my phone won't turn on? +
Bring it in. We specialize in devices that show no signs of life — water damage, hard falls, dead batteries, and more. We'll diagnose it for free and let you know what the options are.
Is there a warranty on repairs? +
All repairs come with a customer satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right with the repair we performed, we'll make it right. Ask us about warranty details when you come in.
Do you buy or sell used phones? +
Yes — we carry pre-owned and refurbished smartphones for sale at great prices. Inventory changes frequently, so call ahead or stop in to see what's available. We also occasionally buy used devices.
